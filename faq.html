About the Foundation
Al-Noman Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization based in Orlando, Florida. Founded in 2021 in memory of Noman Abushanab, who was lost to COVID-19, we empower underprivileged communities worldwide through clean water, education, food security, and places of worship. Our motto is "Be Giving, Be Living."
The foundation was founded in 2021 by the family of Noman Abushanab, who was lost to COVID-19. It was created to honor his memory and continue his spirit of generosity. The name "Al-Noman" means "the blessings" in Arabic.
Yes. Al-Noman Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All donations are 100% tax-deductible. Our EIN is available upon request.
We operate in Benin (West Africa), Palestine, and the United States. The majority of our programs are in Benin, including water well construction, education sponsorship, food distribution, and healthcare programs.

Programs & Campaigns
We run programs across four pillars: Clean Water (building wells in Benin), Education (student and family sponsorship plans), Food Security (Bread a Day, Dabiha meat distribution, Ramadan Iftar meals), and Places of Worship (building and restoring mosques). Visit our Programs page for details.
Water wells start at $500 per well. Each well serves hundreds of people with clean, safe drinking water for years to come. Water wells also qualify as Sadaqah Jariyah (ongoing charity). Learn more.
For as little as 20 cents per person per day, we provide daily bread to families in Benin who cannot afford it. The program runs continuously and feeds hundreds of people. Donate to Bread a Day.
For $30/month, the Student Plan provides one student in Benin with a school bag, food, stationery, shoes, and books — everything they need to attend school and succeed.
For $99/month, the Family Plan supports an entire family in Benin: food, rent, sponsorship for 2 students, parents' utilities, and shoes for the whole family.
