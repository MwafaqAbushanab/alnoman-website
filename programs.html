Our Programs

Building a Better World

Our programs are designed to create lasting, sustainable change in the communities we serve. From clean water to education, every initiative addresses a fundamental human need and empowers people to build brighter futures.

How We Create Lasting Change

Al-Noman Foundation operates six core programs across Benin, Palestine, and beyond. Each program is built on the belief that every person deserves access to clean water, education, food, spiritual spaces, mobility, and safety. More than 90% of every dollar donated goes directly to the people who need it most. We work closely with trusted local partners on the ground to ensure accountability, efficiency, and real impact that transforms lives for generations.

Clean Water

Benin, West Africa

Clean Water for Every Community

Access to clean water is a fundamental human right, yet millions of people in rural Benin still rely on contaminated rivers and ponds for their daily water needs. Waterborne diseases are among the leading causes of illness and death, particularly among children under five. Women and girls spend hours each day walking miles to collect water, time that could be spent in school or earning a livelihood.

Al-Noman Foundation builds water wells in rural communities throughout Benin, providing safe, clean drinking water to hundreds of families with each well. A single well can serve an entire village, dramatically reducing disease, keeping girls in school, and freeing families to focus on building better lives.

  • Each well provides clean water to an entire village
  • Reduces waterborne illness and child mortality
  • Frees girls from water collection so they can attend school
  • Built with local labor, creating jobs in the community

Education

Breaking the Cycle of Poverty

Education That Transforms Lives

Education is the single most powerful tool for breaking the cycle of poverty. In the communities we serve, many children are unable to attend school because their families cannot afford basic supplies like shoes, notebooks, or school bags. Without education, these children face a future of limited opportunity and continued hardship.

Al-Noman Foundation offers two sponsorship plans designed to meet the real needs of students and families. Our goal is not only to put children in classrooms but to create the conditions at home that allow them to stay in school, focus on learning, and build the foundation for a self-sufficient future.

Student Plan

$30/month

Provides a school bag, daily food, stationery, shoes, and textbooks for one student. Everything a child needs to walk into school with confidence and focus on learning.

Family Plan

$99/month

Covers food, rent, two student sponsorships, parents' utilities, and shoes for the entire family. Stabilizes the household so children can stay in school.

Food Security

Feeding Families Every Day

No One Should Go Hungry

Hunger is not just a statistic; it is a daily reality for millions of families who struggle to feed their children even a single meal. In Benin, food insecurity affects rural communities most severely, where farming alone cannot sustain households through drought, floods, or economic hardship. Al-Noman Foundation addresses food security through multiple targeted initiatives that ensure no one in our communities goes without a meal.

Bread a Day

$0.20/day per person

Just 20 cents provides a fresh loaf of bread for one person for an entire day. A $6 donation feeds someone for a full month. Simple, direct, life-sustaining.

Dabiha

Livestock Sacrifice

Our Dabiha program provides fresh meat to families through the sacrifice of livestock. This program allows donors to fulfill their religious obligations while feeding families who rarely have access to protein-rich meals.

Zakat Al-Fitr

Seasonal Giving

Places of Worship

Spiritual Spaces for All

Building Sacred Spaces for Communities

A mosque is far more than a building. It is the spiritual heart of a community, a place where people gather for prayer, learning, celebration, and mutual support. In many underserved communities across Benin, families worship in open fields or crumbling structures that offer little protection from the elements. Children learn the Quran under trees, and community gatherings happen without shelter or dignity.

Al-Noman Foundation is committed to building and restoring mosques in communities that lack proper places of worship. Each mosque we construct becomes a center for spiritual growth, education, and community unity. These are places where marriages are celebrated, the bereaved find comfort, and children discover their faith alongside their neighbors.

  • Build new mosques in underserved communities
  • Restore and renovate existing structures in disrepair
  • Create spaces for prayer, education, and community gatherings
  • Provide dignity and shelter for worshippers year-round

Be Mobile

Restoring Independence

Mobility Is Freedom

For people living with physical disabilities in Benin, the lack of a wheelchair means confinement. It means dependence on others for every basic need, isolation from community life, and the inability to attend school or earn a living. In many rural areas, there is simply no access to mobility equipment, and families cannot afford the cost.

Al-Noman Foundation's Be Mobile program provides wheelchairs to individuals in need throughout Benin. Each wheelchair we deliver represents far more than a piece of equipment; it is the restoration of independence, dignity, and the ability to participate fully in life. Recipients can return to school, rejoin their communities, and move through the world on their own terms.

  • Provides wheelchairs to individuals with mobility challenges in Benin
  • Restores independence and access to education and employment
  • Reconnects people with their communities and daily life
  • Gives dignity and freedom of movement to those who need it most

Humanitarian Aid: Save Gaza

When crisis strikes, Al-Noman Foundation responds. Our Save Gaza campaign provides essential means of life to people in Gaza, Palestine, who face unimaginable hardship every day. Families have been displaced from their homes, children have lost access to schools and medical care, and entire communities struggle to secure food, clean water, and basic supplies.

Through our trusted local partners on the ground, every dollar donated to Save Gaza goes directly toward food packages, clean water, medical supplies, hygiene kits, and shelter materials for families in desperate need. We believe that no political border should determine whether a child eats, whether a mother receives medical care, or whether a family has a roof over their head.

Your donation provides immediate, tangible relief to families who have lost everything. Stand with us in providing hope and the basic means of survival to the people of Gaza.
