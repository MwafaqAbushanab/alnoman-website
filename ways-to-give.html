Employer Match

Does Your Employer Match Donations?

Many companies across the United States match their employees' charitable contributions, effectively doubling or even tripling the impact of your donation at no extra cost to you. This is one of the easiest ways to multiply the good that your generosity does in the world.

If your employer offers a matching gift program, a simple form is all it takes to turn your $30 into $60 or your $99 into $198. Thousands of companies participate, including many Fortune 500 firms.

  • Check with your HR or benefits department to see if your company participates
  • Common matching employers include Google, Microsoft, Apple, Bank of America, and many more
  • Most programs match dollar-for-dollar, and some match up to 3x
  • Submit your matching gift form after donating — it typically takes just a few minutes

Not sure if your company matches? Reach out to us at info@alnoman.org and we will help you find out.

Tax Benefits

Your Donation Is Tax-Deductible

Al-Noman Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, which means every donation you make is fully tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Registered 501(c)(3)

Al-Noman Foundation is recognized by the IRS as a tax-exempt charitable organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

100% Tax-Deductible

Your entire donation is tax-deductible. No goods or services are provided in exchange, so you may deduct the full amount on your federal tax return.

EIN Available

Need our Employer Identification Number for your records? Contact us at info@alnoman.org and we will provide it promptly.

Keep Your Receipt

After each donation, you will receive a receipt via email. Please save this receipt for your tax records as documentation of your charitable contribution.
