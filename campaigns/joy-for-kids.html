Joy for Kids

Toy distribution for underprivileged children

About This Campaign

Every child deserves the simple joy of play. Yet in many underprivileged communities in Benin, children have never owned a toy. The Joy for Kids campaign changes that by distributing toys to children who have so little, bringing smiles and happiness to their lives.

Play is not just fun — it's essential for child development. Through play, children develop motor skills, creativity, social skills, and emotional resilience. A toy can spark imagination and provide comfort to a child who faces daily hardship.

We carefully select age-appropriate, durable toys and organize community distribution events where children receive their gifts. The joy on their faces is priceless, and the impact on their development is lasting.

How Your Donation Helps

  • Age-appropriate toys for children of all ages
  • Educational toys that support learning
  • Community toy distribution events
  • Emotional support through play
  • Childhood joy for those who have so little

Why Give Through Al-Noman Foundation?

More than 90% of every dollar donated goes directly to people in need. As a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit, your donation is 100% tax-deductible. We work with trusted local partners on the ground to ensure aid reaches those who need it most.

Your contribution, no matter the size, makes a real difference. Together, we can bring joy and happiness to the children of Benin.
