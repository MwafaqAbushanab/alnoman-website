Bread a Day

Fresh bread for hungry communities in Benin

About This Campaign

In many parts of Benin, families go to bed hungry every night. For some, even a single piece of bread is a luxury they cannot afford. The Bread a Day campaign aims to change that by providing freshly baked bread to communities in need — every single day.

For as little as 20 cents, you can feed one person a piece of fresh bread. That's less than the cost of a stick of gum. Yet for families living in extreme poverty, it means the difference between going hungry and having sustenance for the day.

Our local partners in Benin bake and distribute bread daily to communities across the country. Your donation directly funds the ingredients, baking, and distribution, ensuring that every cent goes toward feeding those in need.

How Your Donation Helps

  • Daily bread for individuals and families
  • Flour, yeast, and baking supplies for local bakeries
  • Employment for local bakers in Benin
  • Consistent daily nutrition for children
  • Reduced hunger and malnutrition

Why Give Through Al-Noman Foundation?

More than 90% of every dollar donated goes directly to people in need. As a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit, your donation is 100% tax-deductible. We work with trusted local partners on the ground to ensure aid reaches those who need it most.

Your contribution, no matter the size, makes a real difference. Together, we can ensure no one goes to bed hungry.
