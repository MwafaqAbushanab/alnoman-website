Announcing Our 2025 Annual Report

By Mwafaq Abushanab, Founder & Advisor February 15, 2026 5 min read
Back to Blog
Key Takeaways
  • 6 core programs across Benin and Palestine: clean water, education, food security, places of worship, mobility, and humanitarian aid.
  • 10 active campaigns in 2025 — Save Gaza, Bread a Day, Dabiha, Water Wells, Be Mobile, Joy for Kids, Weekly Treats, Hospital Freedom, Ramadan Iftar, and Quran Memorization.
  • 90%+ of every dollar reached programs; 100% volunteer team, no paid staff or executive salaries.
  • Public financial transparency: Form 990-EZ filed with the IRS and listed on ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer; 2026 Candid Silver Seal earned.
  • Looking ahead to 2026: continued Gaza relief, additional water wells in Benin, expanded education sponsorships and Ramadan campaigns.

For the supporting detail: see our Transparency page, Governance page, and Where Does Your Dollar Go?

At Al-Noman Foundation, we believe that transparency is not just a policy — it is a promise. Every donor who entrusts us with their generosity deserves to know exactly where their money goes, who it reaches, and what change it creates. That is why we are proud to present our 2025 Annual Report, a comprehensive look at the work we accomplished together over the past year.

Founded in loving memory of Noman Abushanab, our foundation has grown from a small family-driven initiative into a force for good that serves communities across multiple countries. 2025 was a year that tested our resolve and deepened our commitment to the people we serve. Here is what we achieved — together.

Key Accomplishments in 2025

Thanks to the unwavering support of our donors, volunteers, and partners, Al-Noman Foundation reached significant milestones across all four of our core pillars: clean water, education, food security, and humanitarian relief.

  • 20+ water wells built across rural communities in Benin, providing thousands of people with access to safe, clean drinking water for the first time in their lives.
  • 5,000+ meals distributed through our Bread a Day and Dabiha campaigns, ensuring that families facing food insecurity received nutritious food on a regular basis.
  • 200+ students sponsored in Benin through our education program, covering school supplies, uniforms, books, and tuition so that children can focus on learning instead of worrying about their next meal.
  • Wheelchairs and mobility devices provided through the Be Mobile campaign, restoring independence and dignity to individuals with physical disabilities.
  • Emergency humanitarian aid delivered to families in Gaza through our Save Gaza campaign, including food packages, clean water, medical supplies, and temporary shelter.
"Every well we build, every child we sponsor, every meal we serve is a step toward a world where no one is left behind. This is what your generosity makes possible."

Financial Transparency: Your Dollars at Work

We take pride in our financial discipline. In 2025, more than 90 cents of every dollar donated went directly to our programs and the communities we serve. Our operational overhead remains minimal because of our dedicated volunteer workforce, lean administrative structure, and direct partnerships with trusted organizations on the ground.

We believe donors should never have to wonder whether their contribution made a difference. It did. Every dollar you gave was stretched as far as it could go, and this report is our way of showing you exactly how.

Expanding Our Reach

In 2025, Al-Noman Foundation expanded its operations across seven countries, including Benin, Palestine, Yemen, Turkey, and the United States. Our work in Benin continued to grow with new water wells, expanded student sponsorship programs, and community-driven food initiatives. In Palestine, our Save Gaza campaign responded to the ongoing humanitarian crisis with urgency and compassion.

We also deepened our roots in the United States, engaging local communities through volunteer drives, mosque partnerships, and educational outreach about the global water crisis and food insecurity.

Looking Ahead to 2026

As we step into 2026, we are more determined than ever. Our goals for the year ahead include:

  • Building an additional 30 water wells in underserved areas across West Africa
  • Expanding our student sponsorship program to reach 500 children
  • Launching new campaigns for medical relief and hospital support in Benin
  • Growing our Ramadan giving programs, including Iftar meal sponsorships at $2 per meal
  • Strengthening our crowdfunding platform to empower community-driven campaigns

These goals are ambitious, but with your continued support, they are within reach. Every donation, every share, every prayer moves us closer to a world where no family goes without clean water, food, education, or hope.

Thank You for Being Part of This Journey

None of this would be possible without you. Whether you donated $10 or $10,000, volunteered your time, or simply shared our mission with a friend — you are part of this story. You are the reason a child in Benin can drink clean water for the first time. You are the reason a family in Gaza received a hot meal when they had nothing. You are the reason we exist.

We invite you to continue this journey with us. Read the full report, share it with your network, and consider making a gift today to help us reach even more people in 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I see Al-Noman Foundation's IRS Form 990?

Yes. Our most recent Form 990-EZ is publicly available on ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer and on Candid (formerly GuideStar). Al-Noman Foundation earned the 2026 Candid Silver Seal of Transparency.

How is Al-Noman Foundation governed?

A volunteer Board of Directors oversees the organization. There are no paid staff and no executive salaries; board members serve without compensation. Monthly internal financial reviews monitor income, expenditures, and fund allocation. See our governance page for full details →

Where does Al-Noman Foundation operate?

Programs run primarily in Benin (water wells, education sponsorships, food security, places of worship, and the Be Mobile wheelchair program) and Palestine (Save Gaza humanitarian relief). Al-Noman Foundation is headquartered in Land O' Lakes, Florida, USA, as a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit (EIN 87-1274971).
