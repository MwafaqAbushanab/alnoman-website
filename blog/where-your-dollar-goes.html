Where Does Your Dollar Go? A Transparent Look at Our Spending

January 5, 2026 4 min read
Back to Blog

When you donate to a charity, you deserve to know exactly where your money goes. Is it funding programs on the ground, or is it disappearing into administrative costs, marketing budgets, and executive salaries? At Al-Noman Foundation, we believe transparency is not optional — it is the foundation of trust between us and every person who gives.

We built this organization with a simple promise: your generosity will reach the people who need it most, not get lost in bureaucracy. Here is exactly how we keep that promise.

The 90-Cent Promise

At Al-Noman Foundation, more than 90 cents of every dollar you donate goes directly to our programs and the communities we serve. That means when you give $100, at least $90 is going to build water wells, feed families, sponsor students, provide wheelchairs, or deliver emergency relief to Gaza.

This is not an aspirational goal. It is our track record. We achieve this level of efficiency because of our lean operational model, our dedicated volunteer workforce, and our direct partnerships with trusted organizations in the communities where we work.

"We do not just promise transparency — we practice it. Every dollar entrusted to us carries a responsibility, and we honor that responsibility by making sure it reaches the hands of those who need it most."

Breakdown by Program Area

Your donations fuel real, tangible programs that change lives. Here is how your giving is distributed across our core areas of impact:

Clean Water

Our water wells program in Benin provides entire communities with access to safe drinking water. Each well serves hundreds of people and eliminates the need for women and children to walk miles to find water. Clean water reduces waterborne diseases, increases school attendance, and transforms daily life for families who have never had reliable access before.

Food Security

Through our Bread a Day and Dabiha campaigns, we provide thousands of meals to families facing food insecurity. Bread a Day delivers fresh bread daily for as little as 20 cents per person, while our Dabiha program provides nutritious meat to families who rarely have access to protein. During Ramadan, our Iftar program provides complete meals for just $2 each — rice, salad, a drink, and fish.

Education

Our student sponsorship program covers everything a child needs to succeed in school: tuition, books, uniforms, shoes, school bags, food, and stationery. For $30 a month, you can change the trajectory of a child's life. Our Quran memorization sponsorship program also supports students dedicated to learning and preserving the Quran — and qualifies as a Zakat-eligible contribution.

Emergency Relief

Our Save Gaza campaign responds to the ongoing humanitarian crisis in Palestine with food packages, clean water, medical supplies, and temporary shelter. Every cent donated to this campaign goes directly to emergency relief for displaced families who have lost everything.

How We Keep Overhead Low

Many donors worry about charities with high overhead costs. Here is how Al-Noman Foundation keeps ours to a minimum:

  • Volunteer-driven operations: Our team is largely made up of dedicated volunteers who give their time freely because they believe in the mission.
  • Direct partnerships: We work directly with trusted local organizations in Benin, Palestine, and other regions, cutting out intermediaries and reducing costs.
  • Minimal administrative overhead: We run a lean organization with no lavish offices or executive perks. Every expense is scrutinized to ensure it serves the mission.
  • Digital-first approach: Our website, social media, and digital tools allow us to reach donors and share our impact without expensive marketing campaigns.

What Your Donation Can Do

To give you a clearer picture, here is what specific donation amounts can accomplish through Al-Noman Foundation:

$10

Feeds a person fresh bread for an entire month through the Bread a Day campaign

$30

Sponsors a student for one month — covering school supplies, food, and tuition

$50

Provides 25 complete Iftar meals for families during Ramadan at $2 per meal

$100

Funds emergency medical supplies and food packages for a displaced family in Gaza

These are not hypothetical numbers. They reflect the real cost of delivering real aid to real people. When you donate to Al-Noman Foundation, your money does not get lost in a black box — it arrives in the hands of someone who needs it.

Become a Monthly Donor

One of the most powerful ways to support our work is by becoming a monthly donor. Recurring donations allow us to plan ahead, commit to long-term programs, and ensure consistent support for the families and communities that depend on us. Even $10 a month creates a steady stream of impact that compounds over time.

Whether you choose to give once or give monthly, know this: we take your trust seriously. We will continue to report on how every dollar is spent, share stories from the field, and hold ourselves to the highest standards of accountability. Because that is what you deserve, and that is what the people we serve deserve.
