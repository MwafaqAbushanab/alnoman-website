5 Ways You Can Help Gaza Today

By Mwafaq Abushanab, Founder & Advisor January 20, 2026 4 min read
Back to Blog
Key Takeaways
  • 5 concrete actions: donate, share, organize a fundraiser, educate, and volunteer.
  • Donation impact: $25 = one week of food for a family; $50 = water and hygiene for five families; $100 = critical medical supplies.
  • 90%+ of every dollar reaches families directly through trusted local partners on the ground.
  • Non-financial help matters: sharing, organizing, and volunteering multiply impact when many people participate.
  • Al-Noman Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3), so all donations are tax-deductible.

The humanitarian crisis in Gaza is one of the most pressing challenges of our time. Families have been displaced from their homes, children have lost access to education, and basic necessities like food, clean water, and medical care remain critically scarce. The scale of suffering is staggering, but the power of collective action is even greater.

You do not need to be wealthy, well-connected, or living nearby to make a real difference. There are concrete, practical steps you can take right now — today — to support the people of Gaza. Here are five of them.

1. Donate to the Save Gaza Campaign

The most direct way to help is through financial support. Al-Noman Foundation's Save Gaza campaign channels 100% of your donation toward emergency relief: food packages, clean drinking water, medical supplies, temporary shelter, and hygiene kits for displaced families.

Every dollar counts. A $25 donation can provide an emergency food package for a family for one week. A $50 contribution delivers clean water and hygiene supplies to five families. And $100 funds critical medical supplies for those who have been injured or are in urgent need of care.

"You may feel like one person cannot change the world, but for one family in Gaza, your donation changes everything."

2. Share Awareness on Social Media

Not everyone can donate money, but everyone can share a story. Social media is one of the most powerful tools for raising awareness about the humanitarian crisis in Gaza. A single post can reach hundreds or even thousands of people, inspiring others to take action.

Here is what you can do:

  • Share our campaign page on Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), and LinkedIn
  • Post stories, statistics, and updates about the situation in Gaza
  • Use hashtags to amplify the message and connect with broader movements
  • Tag friends, family members, and community leaders to expand reach

Awareness leads to action. When more people understand the reality on the ground, more people step forward to help.

3. Organize a Community Fundraiser or Drive

Rallying your community is one of the most impactful things you can do. Whether it is a bake sale at your local mosque, a charity dinner, a school fundraiser, or a neighborhood collection drive, community-driven efforts create a ripple effect that multiplies impact far beyond what any one person can achieve alone.

You can also organize a donation matching challenge at your workplace, ask your employer about corporate matching gift programs, or coordinate with local businesses to sponsor a fundraising event. Al-Noman Foundation can provide materials, guidance, and support to help you plan and promote your event.

4. Educate Yourself and Others About the Crisis

Knowledge is the foundation of meaningful action. Take the time to learn about the history, context, and ongoing developments in Gaza from reliable sources. Attend webinars, read reports from human rights organizations, and listen to the voices of people directly affected by the conflict.

Once you are informed, share what you have learned. Start conversations with friends, family, classmates, and coworkers. Write letters to your elected representatives urging them to support humanitarian efforts. When people understand the human cost of this crisis, they are far more likely to act.

5. Volunteer with Al-Noman Foundation

Your time and skills are just as valuable as your financial support. Al-Noman Foundation is always looking for dedicated volunteers to help with fundraising, outreach, social media, event planning, graphic design, translation, and more. Whether you can commit a few hours a week or want to take on a larger role, there is a place for you.

Visit our volunteer page to sign up and learn about current opportunities. Together, we can amplify our impact and reach even more families in need.

The Power of Collective Action

It is easy to feel overwhelmed by the scale of a humanitarian crisis. But history has shown us, again and again, that ordinary people doing small things can create extraordinary change. Every donation, every shared post, every conversation, every hour volunteered adds up to something far bigger than any one of us.

The people of Gaza are not asking for the impossible. They are asking for food, water, medicine, and shelter — the most basic human needs. And we can help provide them. Today. Right now.

Choose one of the five actions above and do it today. Then share this article with someone else and ask them to do the same. That is how movements are built. That is how lives are saved.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much of my donation goes to Gaza relief?

More than 90% of every dollar donated to Al-Noman Foundation goes directly to programs. Save Gaza campaign funds are channeled through trusted local partners on the ground in Gaza for emergency food, water, medical supplies, and shelter. See our full transparency breakdown →

Can I help if I cannot donate financially?

Yes. You can share the campaign on social media, organize a community fundraiser, educate others about the crisis, or volunteer your time with Al-Noman Foundation. Non-financial actions multiply impact when many people participate.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Al-Noman Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization (EIN 87-1274971). You will receive a tax-deductible donation receipt for every contribution, regardless of size.
