## Phase 0 — Legal & Compliance (URGENT)

> These are non-negotiable. Some carry criminal penalties or risk losing your 501(c)(3) status.
> **Do these BEFORE anything else.**

### 0.1 — Verify IRS Form 990 Filings
- [ ] **Action:** Check that ALL annual returns (2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025) have been filed
- **Risk:** Missing 3 consecutive years = **automatic revocation** of 501(c)(3) status
- **How:** Search your EIN at https://apps.irs.gov/app/eos/
- **File type:** 990-N (e-Postcard) if gross receipts under $50K; 990-EZ if $50K–$200K; 990 if over $200K
- **File online:** https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/annual-filing-and-forms
- **Deadline:** May 15 each year (for calendar-year filers)
- **Time:** 30 min per year (990-N), 2-3 hours (990-EZ)

### 0.2 — Florida Charity Registration (FDACS Chapter 496)
- [ ] **Action:** Register with Florida Dept. of Agriculture and Consumer Services
- **Risk:** Soliciting donations in Florida without registration is a **felony**
- **Link:** https://www.fdacs.gov/Consumer-Resources/Charities-Background/Apply-for-Solicitation-of-Contributions
- **Cost:** $10 if under $25K annual revenue; $75 if $25K–$100K; $125 if $100K–$200K
- **Renewal:** 90 days after fiscal year end, annually
- **Time:** 1-2 hours to complete application

### 0.3 — Florida Annual Report (Sunbiz)
- [ ] **Action:** File annual report with Florida Division of Corporations
- **Link:** https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/manage-business/efile/annual-report/
- **Cost:** $61.25/year
- **Deadline:** May 1 each year
- **Risk:** Failure to file = administrative dissolution of the corporation
- **Time:** 15 min

### 0.4 — OFAC Compliance (Palestine Operations)
- [ ] **Action:** Establish written compliance policy for international fund transfers
- **Steps:**
  - [ ] Screen all partners/vendors against the OFAC SDN list: https://sanctionssearch.ofac.treas.gov/
  - [ ] Document all fund usage with receipts and photos
  - [ ] Create a written compliance policy (template: https://www.treasury.gov/resource-center/sanctions/Documents/charity_guidance.pdf)
  - [ ] Keep records of all beneficiaries and distributions
- **Why:** Critical for any nonprofit operating in Palestine. Violations carry severe penalties
- **Time:** Half a day to set up; ongoing diligence

### 0.5 — Multi-State Charitable Solicitation Registration
- [ ] **Action:** Register in states that require it (41 states require registration for online solicitation)
- **Link:** https://www.multistatefiling.org/ (Unified Registration Statement)
- **Priority states:** New York, California, Illinois, New Jersey, Pennsylvania (highest donor populations)
- **Cost:** Varies by state ($0–$300 per state)
- **Tip:** Start with the 5-10 states where you have the most donors, then expand
- **Time:** 2-3 hours per state, or use a service like Harbor Compliance (~$2K/yr for all states)

### 0.6 — Florida Sales Tax Exemption
- [ ] **Action:** Apply for Consumer's Certificate of Exemption (Form DR-5)
- **Link:** https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/sales_tax.aspx
- **Benefit:** Saves 6%+ on all organizational purchases in Florida
- **Cost:** Free
- **Time:** 1 hour

## Phase 1 — Foundation Credibility (Week 1-2)

> These are the gateway steps. Most free programs (Google, Microsoft, Canva) require TechSoup/Percent verification first.

### 1.1 — Verify IRS Exempt Organizations Listing
- [ ] **Action:** Confirm Al-Noman Foundation appears in the IRS EOS database
- **Link:** https://apps.irs.gov/app/eos/
- **Search by:** EIN or organization name
- **Why:** This is the first thing grant makers and donors check
- **Time:** 5 min

### 1.2 — Register with TechSoup / Percent
- [ ] **Action:** Create account and verify nonprofit status
- **Link:** https://www.percent.com (formerly TechSoup verification partner)
- **Also:** https://www.techsoup.org/join
- **What you need:** EIN, 501(c)(3) determination letter, organization info
- **Why:** This is the **master key** — unlocks Google, Microsoft, Canva, and 100+ other programs
- **Processing time:** 2-14 business days for verification
- **Cost:** Free
- **Time:** 30 min to apply

### 1.3 — Claim GuideStar/Candid Profile → Aim for Gold Seal
- [ ] **Action:** Claim your profile and complete it to Gold Transparency level
- **Link:** https://www.candid.org/solutions/update-nonprofit-profile
- **Levels:** Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum
- **What to add:** Mission, programs, leadership, financials, goals, impact metrics
- **Why:** **#1 credibility signal** for institutional donors, grant makers, and foundations. Many won't fund you without a Gold Seal
- **Cost:** Free
- **Time:** 2-3 hours to reach Gold level

### 1.4 — Enroll with PayPal Giving Fund
- [ ] **Action:** Register Al-Noman Foundation with PayPal Giving Fund
- **Link:** https://www.paypal.com/us/webapps/mpp/givingfund/enroll
- **Why:** Required for Facebook/Instagram donation tools (0% processing fees)
- **Prerequisite:** Must be on GuideStar/Candid
- **Cost:** Free (PayPal covers all fees)
- **Time:** 30 min to apply, 2-4 weeks for approval

1. **Start with your GuideStar Gold Seal** — many funders require it
