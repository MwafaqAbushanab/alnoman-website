In Loving Memory

The Story Behind Our Mission

Al-Noman Foundation was founded in remembrance of Noman Abushanab, whom we lost to COVID-19. His spirit of generosity and compassion for others lives on through every well we build, every child we educate, and every family we feed. Our name carries his legacy, and our work honors his memory every single day.

Noman Abushanab Forever in our hearts
Our Values

What Guides Everything We Do

Our values are the foundation of every decision, every project, and every life we touch.

Integrity

We uphold honesty in all of our interactions and hold ourselves to the highest standard of transparency with every dollar donated.

Compassion

We put ourselves in the shoes of our beneficiaries and feel the urgency of their needs, responding with empathy and care.

Togetherness

Our sense of belonging to communities around the world brings us closer to the people in need and strengthens our impact.

Our Promise

More Than 90 Cents of Every Dollar Reaches Those in Need

We operate with radical efficiency. While many organizations spend 30-40% on overhead, we've built Al-Noman to ensure that more than 90% of every dollar you donate goes directly to the communities we serve.

  • Direct Impact More than 90% of every donation funds programs — water wells, food, education, and places of worship.
  • Volunteer-Driven Our team members volunteer their time, keeping administrative costs to an absolute minimum.
  • Registered 501(c)(3) 100% of your donation is tax-deductible. We operate with full legal compliance and accountability.
  • On-the-Ground Presence We work directly with local communities — no middlemen, no wasted resources.
